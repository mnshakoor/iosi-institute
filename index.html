About

An institute-grade learning function for a working intelligence network.

IOSI Global is a society-focused security and intelligence organization operating as a consultancy, an intelligence lab, and a think tank. This portal is its learning function, where the standards behind that work are made teachable and the resources behind stabilization are made usable.

The portal serves two audiences at once. Analysts and officers who need repeatable method, and practitioners in conflict-affected environments who need resources grounded in evidence rather than assertion.

Every track here holds to a single discipline. Claims are traceable, confidence stays proportionate to the evidence, and structure carries the reasoning rather than decorating it.

  • Two curricula: analytic tradecraft and conflict prevention and stabilization.
  • Method aligned to recognized intelligence analytic standards.
  • Applied resources for governance, human rights, and peacebuilding contexts.
  • Operated for the IOSI Global membership network worldwide.

TRACK 01

Analytic Tradecraft

Education for structured, standards-aligned analysis.

Instruction in the methods that make analytic judgments defensible: how to structure reasoning, how to source it, and how to state confidence honestly.

Method

Structured Analytic Techniques

Techniques that externalize reasoning, test assumptions, and reduce the cognitive bias that undermines single-analyst judgment.

Standards

ICD Analytic Standards

Analysis aligned to the Intelligence Community Directive standards for objectivity, sourcing, and the accurate expression of uncertainty.

Warning

WSI

Structured warning and indicators practice: building, weighting, and monitoring the signals that move an assessment before events do.

Framework

Quanta Analytica Process

A framework-governed, LLM-augmented workflow that applies peer-reviewed analytic methods to produce decision-ready intelligence.

TRACK 02

CPS Resources

Conflict Prevention & Stabilization.

Education and applied resources for the people who work to keep conflict from escalating and to help societies recover when it does. Each area below opens onto method, evidence, and practice.

Diagnosis

Conflict Analysis

Reading the drivers, actors, and dynamics of a conflict system before recommending any intervention within it.

Information

Counter-Malign Influence

Identifying and countering influence operations that exploit information environments to deepen instability.

Resolution

Conflict Resolution & Mediation

Structured approaches to de-escalation, negotiation, and mediation between parties in dispute.

Institutions

Governance

How institutional strength, legitimacy, and service delivery shape the trajectory of fragile states.

Protection

Human Rights

The rights frameworks and protection standards that anchor legitimate stabilization and accountability.

Recovery

Peacebuilding

Building the social, political, and economic conditions that hold after a settlement and prevent relapse.
